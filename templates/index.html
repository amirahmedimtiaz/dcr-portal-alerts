      DCR metric guide

      Definitions follow the terminology used by the NISE portal and its official user manual.

      DCR+

      Domestic Content Requirement. The portal traces domestically manufactured solar cells and modules used in applicable government-supported schemes.

      Manufactured+

      The aggregate rated power of solar cells or modules recorded by the portal as manufactured during the selected calendar month.

      Sold+

      The aggregate rated power of solar cells or modules recorded by the portal as sold during the selected month. It is a power quantity, not sales revenue.

      Stock with manufacturer+

      The manufacturer’s portal balance: cell power available for sale or module conversion, and completed modules remaining in stock. It is inventory, not nameplate annual capacity.

      Sold · buyer unclaimed+

      Portal-tracked DCR stock recorded as sold by the manufacturer that the buyer has not yet claimed or credited.

      MW+

      Aggregate rated power of the tracked cells or modules in megawatts. It is not a count of physical pieces.

      Weekly change+

      The difference between consecutive weekly snapshots for a metric’s latest non-zero month. Growth rate is that difference divided by the earlier snapshot value.

      Manufacturer categories+

      The portal classifies registrations as cells only, panels/modules only, or both. The category does not by itself state factory capacity or current stock.
